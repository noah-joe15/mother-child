iPhone or Safari will not open this page? Follow these steps in order:

  1. Switch network. If you are on Wi-Fi, switch to mobile data (or vice versa) and reload. This is the most common fix.
  2. Turn off Private Relay. Settings → tap your name at the top → iCloud → Private Relay → Off.
  3. Turn off Private Browsing in Safari. Open Safari, tap the tabs icon at the bottom, tap "Private", then tap "Done" to return to normal tabs.
  4. Disable experimental QUIC. Settings → Safari → Advanced → Experimental Features → switch OFF anything named HTTP/3 or QUIC.
  5. Update iOS. Settings → General → Software Update. Install any available update, then retry.
  6. Try Chrome or Firefox on the iPhone from the App Store as a temporary alternative.

If none of the above works, your network path to our hosting provider is blocked. Contact the clinic desk for the alternative access link.
