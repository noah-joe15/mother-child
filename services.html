Our RCH Services

Every service found on the physical Kadi ya Kliniki, now digitized, secured, and accessible to every mother and child in Tanzania.

Antenatal Care (ANC)

Four or more focused antenatal visits to monitor the health of mother and baby throughout pregnancy.

Immunization

Complete childhood vaccination schedule — BCG, OPV, Pentavalent, Measles, Rotavirus, and more.

Growth Monitoring

Regular tracking of weight, height, and MUAC to detect malnutrition early and ensure healthy development.

Postnatal Care

Essential care for mother and newborn in the critical days and weeks following delivery.

Family Planning

Counseling and access to safe, modern contraceptive methods for informed reproductive choices.

PMTCT / HIV Care

Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV through testing, counseling, and ARV support.

Malaria Prevention

Distribution of insecticide-treated nets (ITN) and intermittent preventive treatment in pregnancy (IPTp).

Nutrition & Vitamin A

Supplementation and dietary guidance to prevent deficiencies and support child growth.

Deworming

Regular deworming treatment for children to prevent parasitic infections and improve nutrient absorption.

Child Development

Developmental milestone screening to ensure children are meeting key cognitive and physical benchmarks.

Maternal Education

Health education on breastfeeding, hygiene, danger signs, and safe motherhood practices.

Safe Delivery

Skilled birth attendance and emergency obstetric care to ensure safe motherhood for every delivery.
